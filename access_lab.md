# Access workshop lab

We will use a CDE (Cloud Development Environment) for this Formation, you dont have to configure your localhost, everything will run in the browser.

## Getting Started

1. Go to https://coder.el-khayali.com

2. Your identifier for this formation is your lastname in lower case, for composed names use "-" instead of space (Ex: EL KHAYALI => el-khayali)

## Login Credentials

- **Email**: `{YOU-LAST-NAME-LOWERCASE}@gmail.com`
    - *Note: It's not a real mail, its just a random Account name*
    - Example: `el-khayali@gmail.com`
- **Password**: `{YOU-LAST-NAME-LOWERCASE}@2024`
    - Example: `el-khayali@2024`

## Creating Your Workspace

1. In the navigation bar go to **Templates**
2. Click on **kubernetes-docker-dind**
3. Click on **Create Workspace** Button
4. In the **Workspace Name** field, enter: `formation-{YOU-LAST-NAME-LOWERCASE}`
     - Example: `formation-el-khayali`
5. Keep other checkbox as they are
6. Hit **Create Workspace**, and wait for workspace creation
7. Click on **coder-server** to open the IDE that will be used for our training
